# Manager dataset candidates

## Super League sandbox snapshots (3.0)

The optional fictional Super League is playable; this does not unlock the full real English career. Its membership manifest selects the previous completed domestic season, **2025–26**, for a **2026–27** start: six English clubs, four each from Spain/Germany/Italy and two from France. `super-membership.json` retains country, rank, season, source URLs and authored budgets/objectives/priorities. The source season and field remain frozen in each save.

`super-roster-snapshot.json` records fourteen dated UEFA List A snapshots, full names, broad roles and authored positional adaptations. `super-identity-corrections.json` retains primary-source name/birth-date corrections. English clubs reuse the dated FPL candidate and individual authored profiles. These are source snapshots, not proof of full domestic registration, complete youth squads or verified real loan ownership. Initial sandbox ownership is authored; subsequent game transfers and loans use persistent ownership/registration.

Generate `dist/super-catalog.js`, `dist/super-intelligence-data.js` and `super-audit.json` with `node scripts/build-super-catalog.mjs`. The build reads the checked-in membership, roster/correction snapshots, English catalog/reference modules and cached CC0 Transfermarkt CSVs at `work/contracts/players.csv` and `work/contracts/player_valuations.csv`. To obtain those caches on a fresh checkout, first run `node scripts/import-player-intelligence.mjs`; review any refreshed reference changes before regenerating the catalog. Preserve source dates and licences rather than describing the paused dataset as live.

Current audit: **20 field clubs / 517 field players**, **331 foreign players / 317 reference joins**, and **969 unique people** across the wider 34-club world. Nine FPL/UEFA association overlaps are explicitly resolved using the selected active snapshot. Twelve birth dates and fourteen foreign reference joins remain unknown. Published wages/contracts/values remain per-field dated references; missing facts are not fabricated. Potential and starting ownership/economy remain game estimates. See [3.0 provenance, compatibility and verification](../../docs/RELEASE-3.0.md).

## Player intelligence references (2.9)

`player-intelligence.json` and `player-intelligence-audit.json` are generated by `node scripts/import-player-intelligence.mjs`. The browser module is `dist/player-intelligence-data.js`. These are per-field dated references, not a verified current-world registration dataset. The bulk Transfermarkt dataset is paused at 6 July 2026 and declares CC0; its licence is retained under `licenses/`. Newer official contract announcements and individual public wage estimates are recorded with source/date/precision in `player-intelligence-overrides.json`. Exact/unambiguous identity matching leaves 76 players unmatched. Never infer a missing exact contract expiry, salary, release clause or factual potential score.

See [sources, coverage, GBP conversion and limitations](../../docs/RELEASE-2.9.md). Published value estimates are distinct from asking prices. Reported wages remain estimates. Potential ranges are authored Touchline scouting projections. These references do not unlock the full real English career gates below.

These files are verification inputs, not unlocked gameplay worlds. `england-2026-candidate.json` comes from the dated official Fantasy Premier League public feed. Its coverage is the fantasy player pool and does not establish complete registered academy squads. Attribute values are authored Touchline game profiles, not EA ratings. Missing birth dates remain null.

`england-2026-overrides.json` contains individually sourced corrections. In 2.7.1 it verifies two missing birth dates and records Mheuka's Celtic loan separately from his Chelsea parent association. Twelve dates remain missing. Feed association is not proof of active registration: 667 players still require an active-club audit. The exact loan return date is not inferred from “season-long.” `fa-cup-2026-round-dates.json` contains official planning dates only and cannot unlock a cup.

`england-2026-validation.json` lists the current release blockers. Run `node scripts/import-manager-england.mjs` from the project root to refresh both files from their named official source. The runtime validator in `dist/manager-datasets.js` rejects incomplete league membership, duplicate identities, missing source/age/profile data and insufficient positional cover. Calendar, cup and ownership checks are additional prerequisites for a real career release.

## Squad acquisition-cost benchmark

`squad-cost-benchmark.json` records the 20 Premier League clubs from the dated CIES 9 September 2026 acquisition-cost study, with source URLs, cohort selection, mean/median and the frozen GBP conversion. The source ENG filter includes lower divisions, which are excluded. These fees include possible add-ons; they are neither market values nor annual squad costs or club purchase prices. The authored new-save funding policy is in `dist/club-funding.js`; takeover budgets are generated by `scripts/build-english-sandbox.mjs`. See [2.9.1 research and compatibility](../../docs/RELEASE-2.9.1.md).
