# Research data

`manager/` contains dated football-manager research candidates, source captures, review ledgers and historical audits. These records are retained for provenance; they are not a verified current registrations, contracts or valuation database.

The playable application serves its catalogs and assets from `dist/`. The separately sourced analytics artifacts are under `dist/analytics-data/`, with pinned original-source hashes and CC BY 4.0 attribution. Their pipeline is in [scripts/analytics](../scripts/analytics/), and their interpretation is documented in [the analytics guide](../docs/ANALYTICS.md).

Raw analytics source downloads, personal saves and local caches are not committed here. Raw downloads are reproduced into ignored `work/analytics-source/`.
