## What this exposes about the game

- Optional recruitment delegation is implemented with explicit spending/wage limits. This run has delegation off. Opponent recruitment uses current positions, cooldowns, six months of wage reserves and an annual affordability check.
- Fast-career-2 produces modeled goals, assists and full-match starting-XI minutes. Live-match events remain recorded data; unknown live assists stay unknown. Player statistics reconcile to team goals, with league and cup separated.
- Opening ownership, loans, contracts and financial inputs are not a verified August dataset. The verified-career gate remains blocked.
- Income is authored and date-based, not a complete attendance/sponsorship/prize/cost economy. Match balance and contract/wage assumptions need calibration.
- Further game work: weekly decisions and consequences; stronger opposition planning; football rules, injuries/suspensions and season-world depth; physical-device usability and balancing; install/update/save recovery before optional accounts.

No application code, live browser progress or user-owned saved career was changed by these simulations.
