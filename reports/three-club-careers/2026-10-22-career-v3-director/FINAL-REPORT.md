## Verification and limits

All 33 CSV exports reconcile to their structured reports. Three seasons complete 1,140 league fixtures and 57 cup fixtures, with zero same-day transfer cycles. The league remains a fixed-membership fictional Super League; these are not Spanish or English domestic season simulations. New fast matches model substitutions and all-club fatigue; injuries, cards and physical match ratings are not invented. Published source terms and mutable game terms remain separate.
