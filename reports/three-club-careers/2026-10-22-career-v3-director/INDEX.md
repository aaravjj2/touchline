## What this exposes about the game

- Optional recruitment delegation is implemented with explicit spending/wage limits. This run has delegation enabled. Opponent recruitment uses current positions, cooldowns, six months of wage reserves and an annual affordability check.
- Fast-career-3 produces modeled goals, assists, substitutions and reconciled minutes. Every club carries fatigue between fixtures. Live-match events remain recorded data; unknown live assists stay unknown. Player statistics reconcile to team goals, with league and cup separated.
- Opening ownership, loans, contracts and financial inputs are not a verified August dataset. The verified-career gate remains blocked.
- Income is authored and date-based, not a complete attendance/sponsorship/prize/cost economy. Match balance and contract/wage assumptions need calibration.
- Remaining game work: verified opening-day data; cards/injuries/suspensions and football-rule parity; economic and human game-feel balance; physical-device/cross-browser validation. Offline caching, deferred updates and recovery are implemented locally.

No application code, live browser progress or user-owned saved career was changed by these simulations.
