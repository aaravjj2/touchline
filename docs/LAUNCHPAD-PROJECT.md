# Touchline — Your Club. Your Call.

Build a club, play the match, and revisit your decisions in a browser football sandbox.

Football fans often choose between club management and directly playing matches. Touchline connects those decisions in one local browser career. Review the next fixture, diagnose natural-position cover, prepare a lineup, then play yourself, manage live or simulate. Recorded inputs support replays and Decision Lab branches; local recovery and portable exports retain progress.

The prototype uses vanilla JavaScript, Three.js, web workers and local browser storage. Vercel serves the public app; the optional local ZIP needs only Node.js 24. No account or paid inference service is required.

Codex assisted implementation, debugging, UI inspection, testing and documentation under human product direction. The game has no live AI. This is an existing project: see the [contribution ledger](LAUNCHPAD-CONTRIBUTIONS.md).

The work demonstrates a connected football-management and direct-play loop, not evidence of proven enjoyment or competitive economy balance. Real-world data is an explicitly unverified sandbox, financial terms include authored estimates, and the verified opening-day career remains locked. Photo coverage is incomplete and labelled. Controller support, cloud accounts and multiplayer are outside this build.
