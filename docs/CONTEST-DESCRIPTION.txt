Touchline is a football sandbox where you manage a club, draft your dream XI, play matches, build a player career and revisit recorded decisions. Challenge a friend in Draft Duel: join with a room code, take turns picking unique players, choose hidden tactics and share one match result. Play from a mobile phone or laptop with no account or install. Solo progress stays on your device; shared rooms reconnect across sessions. Built with ChatGPT, with clear rules, live updates and offline solo play.
